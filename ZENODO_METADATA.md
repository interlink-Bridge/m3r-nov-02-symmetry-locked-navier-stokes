## Description

This public technical note studies an explicit smooth periodic divergence-free family for the three-dimensional incompressible Navier–Stokes equations and isolates a symmetry point at which the resolved heat-filtered strain and the deviatoric caloric covariance are constrained to opposite traceless diagonal shapes.

For every fixed positive heat parameter, the preserved symmetry yields a nonzero heat-parameter-dependent physical-time interval on which the normalized favorable stress–strain alignment remains exactly saturated, $\chi=1$. Independently, a kinematic saturation lemma gives $\dot\chi=0$ at every sufficiently regular saturated state.

The note distinguishes two observation protocols that must not be conflated:

1. a calorically matched path $\tau(t)=T-t$; and
2. physical-time differentiation at fixed positive heat scale.

For the matched path, exact finite Fourier algebra yields a family-specific transfer-threshold function with a unique interior minimizer. Exact Sturm analysis and rational enclosure certify

For the fixed-scale protocol, an independent exact control yields a distinct threshold that is strictly decreasing on $0<q<1$ with limiting infimum $3$.

Consequently, within the declared explicit family, symmetry-locked alignment geometry, local strain growth, and favorable cross-scale transfer growth are not interchangeable dynamical statements.

The package includes reproducible symbolic verification of divergence freedom, pressure-Poisson compatibility, pressure Hessian, strain derivative, heat-covariance formulas, matched-path derivative, fixed-scale derivative, exact Sturm root count, and certified threshold enclosure.

### Claim boundary

This work is family-specific and local in physical time. It does not establish long-time persistence, finite-time singularity formation, global Navier–Stokes regularity, or a Millennium Prize solution. The value $R_{\min}$ is not a universal Reynolds-number threshold. No heat-scale-uniform persistence interval is claimed. The manuscript is not peer reviewed and independent external line-by-line certification is not claimed. Worldwide novelty and priority over unidentified prior art are not claimed.
