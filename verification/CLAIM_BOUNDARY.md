## Theorem-facing claims

For the explicit smooth periodic divergence-free family

$$
u_A=A\bigl(-\sin x(\cos y+\cos z),\;\cos x\sin y,\;\cos x\sin z\bigr),
$$

and its unique local smooth periodic incompressible Navier–Stokes solution:

1. The declared symmetries force the resolved heat-filtered strain and deviatoric caloric covariance at the origin into opposite traceless diagonal shapes.
2. For each fixed heat parameter $\tau>0$, there exists $\varepsilon_\tau>0$ such that favorable normalized alignment remains exactly saturated, $\chi_\tau=1$, on $0\le t<\varepsilon_\tau$. No heat-scale-uniform persistence interval is claimed.
3. At any $C^1$ saturated state, $\dot\chi=0$ is a kinematic identity. Under $C^2$ regularity, the displayed second-derivative identity is also valid.
4. Along the explicitly declared matched caloric path $\tau(t)=T-t$, the initial transfer derivative is classified by the exact threshold $\mathcal R_*(q)$ with a unique interior minimizer $q_*$ and certified enclosure
   $$
   0.63651056784173490941<q_*<0.63651056784173490942
   $$
   and
   $$
   5.78812722135752519109224751394<R_{\min}<5.78812722135752519155412626161.
   $$
5. At fixed heat scale, the initial transfer derivative has the distinct exact threshold $\widehat{\mathcal R}(q)$, with $\widehat{\mathcal R}'(q)<0$ on $(0,1)$ and endpoint limits $+\infty$ and $3$.
6. The interval
   $$
   3<\frac{A}{\nu}<R_{\min}
   $$
   is an exact matched-path strain/transfer separation regime. It is not an all-heat-scale fixed-scale separation regime.
